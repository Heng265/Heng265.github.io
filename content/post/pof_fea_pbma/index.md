🧪 **New publication alert!**  
I'm excited to share that our review article, titled “Finite element analysis as a promising approach for texture development of plant-based meat analogs,” has been published in *[Physics of Fluids](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/pof/article/37/3/031302/3339150)*.

In this work, we explore how finite element analysis (FEA) can help model and predict the mechanical and thermodynamic behaviors during the processing of plant-based meat analogs (PBMAs). Achieving the fibrous, meat-like texture of animal protein is a major challenge in food design. We show that FEA, especially when combined with advanced rheological models and coupled multi-physics simulations, can replicate complex texture formation in extrusion, shear cell, and 3D printing processes.

This review bridges knowledge from food science, soft matter physics, and computational mechanics, and provides a roadmap for using FEA to accelerate innovation in sustainable protein production.

Thanks to my co-author Jingnan Zhang and everyone supporting this interdisciplinary work. I'm happy to connect with others working on computational food design, alternative proteins, and soft matter modeling!
